A city should help you live. Not just exist.

Every person deserves a safe, healthy, accessible, sustainable and accountable civic environment — and government should have a measurable duty to provide it.

11 things every city should get right.

02 — The core mechanism

Right → Duty → Standard → Accountability.

A right becomes meaningful when it creates a duty, the duty has a measurable standard, and failure has a transparent path to correction.

03 — Measure the city

The Right to Live Index.

Cities should be judged by the conditions people experience — not only by projects announced or money spent.

Possible measures include safe mobility, walkability, air quality, water and sanitation, public transport, public-space access, civic response times and accountability.
